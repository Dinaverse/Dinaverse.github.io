About

Who's behind this lab.

Student in the AEC Infrastructure Informatique et Cybersecurite program at College de Bois-de-Boulogne, in Montreal. The lab documented on this site is both a learning environment and a record of real, running infrastructure. Nothing here is a tutorial exercise that gets torn down afterward.

Before starting formal study, I had already spent 10 years self-taught in networking, systems administration, and computer hardware repair, the kind of infrastructure work most people only meet in a classroom. The AEC program gave that experience a name and a methodology; the home lab is where I keep testing it against real failures instead of lab-manual ones.

I learn by building and I document as I go, which is why the Projects page reads more like a running log than a portfolio showcase: real postmortems, real configs, real repos. If something broke and I fixed it, it's written down.
